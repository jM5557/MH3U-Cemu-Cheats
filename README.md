Cheats can be added and enabled on the following custom Cemu build (REQUIRED): 
- PC (Linux/SteamOS/Windows): https://github.com/jM5557/Cemu-Enhanced
- Android: https://github.com/jM5557/Cemu-Android-MH3U-Revival

Note: Always make a backup copy of your save data before enabling new or untested cheats!

# Compatibility
- Monster Hunter 3 Ultimate v32 EUR (Title ID: 0005000010117200)

# Installation
- Download `0005000010117200.txt`
- Open your Cemu folder and place `0005000010117200.txt` inside the `cheats` folder
- In Cemu desktop launch the game and click "Tools" > "Cheats" on the top navigation bar OR in Cemu Android press and hold on the game > "Cheats"

Note: Quality of Life (QOL) improvements named below are already enabled by default

# QOL Improvements
- Full Equipment Box in Port Tanzia - Change equipment, appearance, and equipment sets directly from Port Tanzia's Tavern item box.
<br>Works perfectly when playing offline. When playing in multiplayer, for others to see your newly equipped gear, you must exit and re-enter the Tavern (can simply move to the Marina and then back to the Tavern without disconnecting from the Lobby).

- Hold "A" to Carve/Mine/Gather - Hold the "A" button when carving or gathering the same as in modern MH titles

- Smooth vertical camera (underwater-style) - Tilting the camera on land vertically up or down is now smooth instead of limited (adapted from the underwater camera controls)

- Skip Quest End Timer (Hold X+Y) - Allows you to skip the end of quest timer (usually 1 minute) after clearing a quest (similar to Monster Hunter Wilds). Skipping takes you to the rewards screen quicker. Press and hold the X and Y buttons together for roughly ~1 second to activate.

- Send All Rewards To Item Box (Hold X+Y) - Allows you to send all rewards to item box by holding X and Y (similar to new games that have that as an option).

# Experimental
- Free camera in towns - Moga Village and Port Tanzia hubs use the in-hunt camera with full controls (rotate, tilt).

- Frame cap 44 FPS - Locks the in-game framerate to ~44 FPS allowing for several in-engine timings to work correctly (affects hammer golf-swing, lance charge). Adapts raccu.com's Classic Hardware Sync originally for MH3U for 3DS

# Cheats (Not recommended for Online Play)
- Many Zenny (999,999z) - Changes your Zenny amount to 999,999. For any other amount change `000F423F` in the cheat code to a numeric decimal value converted to hexadecimal.
